### You will need:
- Breadboard
- 2 x LED
- 2 x 555 timer
- 4 x 47 kΩ resistors
- 2 x 470 Ω or 330 Ω resistor
- 2 x 10 µF capacitor
- Jumper cables
- 9V battery
- PP3 connector (battery snap)
- Prototype circuit board
- Conductive thread
- Needle
- A pair of gloves
- Soldering kit

**Optional**:
- Potentiometer
- Light dependent resistor (LDR)
- Extra 47 kΩ resistors
