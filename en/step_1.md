### You will need:
- Breadboard
- LED
- 1 x 555 timer
- 2 x 47k resistors
- 1 x 470Ω or 330Ω resistor
- 1 x 10uF capacitor
- Jumper cables
- 9V battery
- PP3 connector (battery snap)
