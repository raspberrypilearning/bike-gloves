### You will need:
- Breadboard
- 2 LEDs
- 2 555 timers
- 4 47kΩ resistors
- 2 470Ω or 330Ω resistors
- 2 10µF capacitors
- Jumper wires
- 9V battery
- PP3 connector (battery snap)
- Prototype circuit board
- Conductive thread
- Needle
- A pair of gloves
- Soldering kit

**Optional**:
- Potentiometer
- Light-dependent resistor (LDR)
- Extra 47kΩ resistors
